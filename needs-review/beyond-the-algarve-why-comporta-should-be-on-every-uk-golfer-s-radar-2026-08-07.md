For most UK golfers, a Portugal golf trip means the Algarve. Vale do Lobo, Quinta do Lago, Vilamoura. They are well-established, easy to book, and genuinely good. But familiarity has its costs: crowded tee sheets in peak season, green fees that have crept steadily upward, and a trip that starts to feel like a well-worn formula. If you are ready to look elsewhere, Comporta should be on your radar.

## What Is Terras da Comporta?

The Terras da Comporta resort sits on Portugal's Atlantic coast, roughly an hour to an hour and twenty minutes south of Lisbon Airport, on the edge of the Sado Estuary Nature Reserve. The landscape is unlike anywhere else in Portugal: umbrella pines, wide Atlantic dunes, unspoiled coastline, and a quietness the Algarve lost some years ago.

The resort is now home to two championship courses, opened in consecutive years. That combination, two distinct world-class layouts on the same stretch of coastline, is what makes the case for a dedicated golf trip genuinely compelling rather than just interesting on paper.

## The Dunas Course: Proper Links Golf, in Portugal

The Dunas Course opened in 2023 and was designed by David McLay-Kidd, whose portfolio includes Bandon Dunes in Oregon and the Castle Course at St Andrews. That pedigree tells you something about the ambition here.

It plays as a par-71 over more than 7,200 yards from the back tees, built across 84 hectares of sandy terrain with festuca grass fairways. McLay-Kidd has described it as a true links layout, and by most accounts it earns that description in a way no other course in Portugal currently does. It runs alongside Praia do Pego beach, and the combination of open Atlantic exposure, firm fairways, and natural dune topography gives it a character that stands apart from anything you will find in the Algarve.

Since opening it has been recognised widely as one of the finest courses in continental Europe. That is not the resort's marketing department talking; it has appeared on several credible pan-European course rankings. For UK golfers who travel to Ireland or Scotland chasing genuine links golf, the Dunas Course is worth paying attention to.

## The Torre Course: A Strong Debut from Garcia

The Torre Course opened in the second half of 2025, and it arrived with immediate recognition. It was named the 2025 World's Best New Golf Course at the World Golf Awards.

It is the first full course design from Sergio Garcia, and the influences he drew on are clear. Inspired in part by Valderrama, the layout is built around strategic, smaller greens and a set of varied par threes. It stretches to around 6,900 yards from member tees and can be extended beyond 7,300 yards. It covers 41.4 hectares of natural landscape.

Where the Dunas Course is wide and links-influenced, the Torre offers something more measured and strategic. Two courses on the same trip that play completely differently, on the same stretch of coast, is a genuine asset rather than a marketing convenience.

## The Practical Case for Booking Comporta

**The flight is easy.** UK golfers fly into Lisbon, which has strong direct connections from most major UK airports. You are not adding time by avoiding Faro; in fact for many UK cities Lisbon is the more convenient option.

**The Lisbon bolt-on is a real differentiator.** A night or two in Lisbon either side of the golf makes obvious sense, and it is a city that rewards the visit. The Algarve does not offer an equivalent; it is essentially a pure golf and beach destination. Comporta gives you options.

**The courses are different from each other.** A common weakness of multi-course trips is spending a week playing variations on the same parkland theme. Dunas and Torre are genuinely distinct experiences.

**Green fees.** No verified published rates were available at the time of writing, so it would not be fair to quote specific figures. What is reasonable to say is that golf in this part of Portugal is positioned at the premium end, in keeping with the quality of the product. If the Algarve's top courses at peak season already sit comfortably above €150 a round, factor accordingly when budgeting a Comporta trip. Contact the resort directly for current pricing.

**Where to stay.** Sublime Comporta is the frequently cited accommodation option in the area, a 65-acre retreat with 23 rooms and suites. It suits the tone of the region: low-key, unhurried, not a branded golf hotel in the conventional sense.

## Who This Trip Is For

Comporta is not yet on the package-holiday circuit in the way the Algarve is, which is precisely the point. Golfshake's travel team called out the Comporta cluster as the destination for 2026 for golfers who want to get somewhere before the crowds follow. That window will not stay open indefinitely.

If you have done the Algarve a few times and want something that feels less predictable, two world-class courses on an unspoiled stretch of Atlantic coast, a short drive from one of Europe's best city-break destinations, is a hard argument to dismiss. Start making enquiries now if 2026 is on the table, and 2027 at the latest if you need more time to plan.
